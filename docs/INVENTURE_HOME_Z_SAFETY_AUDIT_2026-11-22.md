# Inventure Home Z safety audit, 22 November 2026

Two physical runs established a repeatable T0 release height. Later runs stopped at two different stages. In the run immediately after the successful 200 °C wipe, Klipper stopped during heating. In an earlier run the STM32 exhausted the first-contact search. The heater failure is reproduced on MCU R14 and corrected in MCU R15. The reason the nozzle-to-pad circuit failed to close in time on the printer remains open.

This audit covers the Orange Pi R38.4 operator route, the field MCU R14 and the R15 heater fix at commit `943059e`. The records below are from the Inventure. Times are approximate, in Europe/Warsaw. Repeated copies of a log and repeated messages from a single shutdown count as one event.

## What the button actually runs

1. Fluidd Home Z, the `INVENTURE_HOME_Z` macro and the OLED reach the operator homing route. Confirmation covers the seated tray, clear pad and physically lowered T0. The host obtains native control after checking the printer is idle and the MCU permits it. Native Home uses the selected T0. It does not operate the mechanical selector or wipe the nozzle. The separately completed CLEAN has its own selection, brush and bin stages.
2. The table goes down to the lower switch PE12. For Home Z, the host checks the current XY reference again after confirmation. If either axis is unknown, it homes Y then X. Home All requests both. The carriage moves over the configured X82.8 Y68.0 pad while the table is at the bottom. T0 heats to the configured 200 °C.
3. The table rises slowly until PE12 releases, takes a separate 1 mm clearance move, then rises at the field setting of 8 mm/s to nominal Z2. PC14 already closed at the bottom or closing on the coarse approach stops the sequence.
4. The STM32 takes exclusive control of STEP/DIR for the fine approach. One upward step every 4 ms gives about 0.312 mm/s. After detecting a closure it reverses to find electrical release. It retains the best release and normally settles after 101 consecutive cycles without improvement. A previously measured best release can also be used when the upward travel bound is reached. Without one, the run fails. Only a verified release can establish the working zero. The outer sequence then parks at Z10.

The five-point leveling procedure, the two-nozzle pad scan at 100 °C and its XY edge tolerance are separate consumers of this hardware. Their temperature bands and XY tolerances do not govern native Home Z. ## What can stop the run

| Check | Where it applies | Effect and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Idle, confirmation, motion ownership and coordinates | Host before motion and before taking the local Z reference | An active operation, unresolved fault, invalid working frame or wrong commanded pad XY can refuse the request. The confirmation acknowledges the physical T0 selection. The software cannot sense its mechanical position through this check. |
| Lower switch PE12, tray PC13, contact PC14 and Z enable PE10 | Before lateral movement, on coarse motion and during the local measurement | The tray must read 0. At the bottom PE12 must read 1 and PC14 must be open (raw 1). PE12 must release and remain clear for fine probing. MCU supervision checks the tray and the shared driver enable. A qualified PC14 closure has raw level 0. |
| Heater gain during warm-up | Klipper `verify_heater extruder` | The field profile uses Klipper defaults, including an initial 2 °C gain over a 20 s check window and cumulative error handling. This was the first shutdown after the wipe. A requested duty of 40% in the log records a command to the timer. |
| Native probing temperature | Host and MCU, separate from heater-gain verification | For the 200 °C target the MCU requires a fresh T0 sample from 190 °C inclusive to 208 °C exclusive. The host waits for the heater controller to settle. Its repeated in-motion check includes the upper endpoint, while the MCU independently applies its exclusive bound. The matched `no contact` record came from the travel branch. |
| Heartbeat, cable PD11, ADCs, cooling and doors | MCU independently of Fluidd | Heartbeat expires at 5 s, monitored ADC samples must be younger than 1 s, and loss of required fan tach edges for 10 s can trip native control. Door and lid contacts apply when the global door policy is enabled. The logs above do not identify any of these as the trigger in the two fully diagnosed attempts. |
| Travel, refinement cycles, phase timers and ownership handoff | Host and MCU | The default local search has 8020 steps of maximum net upward travel (10 mm), a 512-cycle ceiling and a 600 s MCU time ceiling. The host also bounds heat waiting (300 s by default), the reference (600 s), and individual phases. The MCU allows 10 s for the final ownership handoff. Host progress reports every 5 s. The 01:15 counters identify the travel limit before a first qualified contact. |

The nominal field Z2 start and 10 mm local budget can command upward motion past the working coordinate Z0 if the contact stays open. In the local MCU mode the host checks the start against the configured lower software coordinate limit. The local search then enforces its own step budget. Increasing this budget would increase unverified upward travel. A native terminal failure ends pulses and invokes `trip(8)`, which latches a local shutdown. An interrupted host query can then display `No communication with MCU`. Conversely, in the heater incident the host initiated shutdown and the MCU recorded `Command request`. The first event and the terminal MCU record distinguish these cases. ## Findings from code and replay

- **R14 heater ownership defect.** In an identical service heat, output release, native selection and 40% heat request, both builds reported native duty 102 and timer compare 154. R14 left T0 PA0 in GPIO mode 1 with the active-low heater off. R15 restored the timer output (mode 2) with compare 256, the safe OFF value, before accepting the new duty. This explains why the same nozzle could heat for cleaning and cool under the following native heat request. The R15 regression also checks the other configured heater channels and interlocks. [R15 fix and field check](INVENTURE_HEATER_HANDOFF_R15.md)
- **What “no contact” proves.** A register-level replay on the actual MCU source lowered PC14 for the first time at step 8019, then in a separate test at step 8020. At 8019 the controller reversed direction. At 8020 it reported `no contact` with 8020 up and 0 down, even with PC14 now low. Holding PC14 open throughout gave the same counters. Stock r0323 checks the positive travel bound at `0x0801DC94–0x0801DC96` before sampling the contact at `0x0801DC98`. This accounts for exactly one endpoint step (0.00125 mm). It does not explain why the field run failed to qualify a contact near its previously measured height. The physical tool position, actual table travel and electrical continuity at that instant are unresolved.
- **A diagnostic gap before probing.** A reproduced host heat-wait timeout left T0 target zero and cleared the temporary Z reference, but `last_result` was null because the inner probe routine had never started. After a firmware reset the retained STM32 record also disappears. A null status after restart cannot identify the preceding failure. For a local shutdown, R38.4 can read the owner, token and counters before reset. For a warm-up shutdown, the first Klipper shutdown message is the primary record.
- **An additional preflight restriction.** The host rejects any registered motion transform. In the project's pinned Klipper, a configured bed mesh registers a transform even with an empty mesh. Clearing a mesh leaves that transform installed. The examined Inventure field configuration contains no `[bed_mesh]` section and reached the pad. This restriction matters if a mesh is later added.

## Verification and remaining physical check

On the audited commit, **821 host tests and all eight MCU register-test programs passed**. The pinned Klipper also loaded the upgraded and clean profiles and exercised the installed Home buttons, confirmation and park with a simulated MCU. In this route test nozzle temperature follows the simulated target and software supplies the contact. Those tests establish routing and control logic. The two November physical references establish that the mechanism worked under the recorded conditions.

The latest supplied printer log still identifies MCU R14. To distinguish the two remaining hardware questions without another blind Home, first verify the installed MCU with `INVENTURE_VERSION`. On R15 the existing [stationary post-wipe heating check](INVENTURE_HEATER_HANDOFF_R15.md#check-heating-above-the-waste-bin) isolates T0 reheating over the bin. Independently, on a cold, stationary printer, read `INVENTURE_INPUTS` with the nozzle-to-pad electrical path open, temporarily connected at those two exposed metal surfaces, and open again. The expected sequence is `raw.nozzle_contact = 1, 0, 1`, with `raw.tray = 0`. A successful static check confirms the input circuit under those conditions. Verifying the actual travel and physical T0 alignment at contact remains a separate observation. The audit delivered documentation only.
